Three islands, three different days

Grinda

About an hour and a half by boat from the city. Small enough to walk around in a day. Bring good shoes, the paths are rocky.

Utö

Iron ore was mined here for hundreds of years and the old mine holes are still there, full of water. Rent a bike at the harbour, the island is big.

Sandhamn

The sailors’ island. Busy in summer, so go on a weekday if you want some peace and quiet.
